# Changelog

## v1.2.0 — 2026-11-06

### Play Builder

#### Added

- Lock the animation doc to remember its position and width in this browser.

#### Changed

- Video and GIF downloads hold the final frame for an extra second.
- A little more space beside the playback timeline makes the time easier to read.

#### Fixed

- Movement paths stay visible when switching between steps.

### Across the App

#### Added

- A What’s New page with brief updates grouped by area.
- Click the version number in any footer to read the latest changes.

#### Fixed

- Removed a thin background gap beneath the page header.

## v1.1.0 — 2026-11-05

Our first numbered release brings together the latest Play Builder improvements.

### Play Builder

#### Added

- Animate plays with steps, custom paths, and timing controls.
- Present plays in full screen with a laser pointer or spotlight.
- Signed-in users can download animations as videos or GIFs.

#### Changed

- Move, copy, delete, and restore groups of elements together.
- Smoother color changes and clearer playback controls.

#### Fixed

- More reliable step navigation, selection, and spotlight behavior.

### Case Plays

#### Fixed

- Case-play results fit better on smaller screens.

### Across the App

#### Added

- The app version appears beside Your Privacy Choices in the footer.
