FEATURE VERIFICATION NOTES

Audit date: July 28, 2026
Production site: https://caseplay.org/
Production builder: https://caseplay.org/play-builder
Test viewport: 1440 × 900

CASEPLAY.ORG WORKFLOW

1. Searchable case-play database
   Location: CasePlay.org home page, “Search case plays...” field.
   Result: Successfully tested with the search term “roughing.” Matching case-play links carried the search value in the q URL parameter.
   Limitation/uncertainty: The visible result-count text still read “10 case plays found” during this particular query, so the article does not make a claim about live count updates.

2. Difficulty filtering
   Location: Home page, “Filter Difficulty” button.
   Result: Successfully opened. Easy, Moderate, and Hard options were visible.
   Limitation/uncertainty: The article mentions searchable case plays but does not rely on or describe the difficulty filter.

3. Case-play prompt and spoiler ruling
   Location: A live case-play page for “Completed Pass, Roughing & Taunt.”
   Result: Successfully inspected the visible prompt, author/source metadata, rule reference, page number, black answer area, reveal control, and similar-case-play column. Clicking the reveal control successfully exposed the ruling text.
   Limitation/uncertainty: None relevant to the article.

ARTICLE ACCURACY BOUNDARIES

• The article describes the builder as a diagramming, teaching, presentation, and discussion tool.
• It does not claim that the builder generates rulings, simulates plays, verifies rule enforcement, or automatically attaches diagrams to case plays.
• The example is deliberately described as a neutral illustration, not as a rules case with a verified ruling.
• The article clearly states that the builder is in early development and that controls, workflows, and visual details may change.
